🍏 For iOS / iPhone (Deep Account Cache Flush)

If it's rolling back on an iPhone, it's almost always a stuck cloud purchase flag tied to Family Sharing or App Store queues:

  • Go to Settings > [Your Name] > Media & Purchases, sign out, restart your phone, and sign back in.
  • Tap any tab in the App Store app (like Today or Search) 10 times fast to clear the local App Store cache.
